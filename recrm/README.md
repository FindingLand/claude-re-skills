# recrm — Build Your Own Real Estate CRM in Airtable

Turns Claude into your CRM builder. Claude sets up the tables, fields, linked records, and views a real estate business actually needs: leads/contacts, properties, a deal pipeline with kanban, and follow-up views, then connects your lead sources.

You own it and it costs about $0 to $20 a month, instead of renting a CRM for $69 to $1,500 a month.

**[Download recrm.skill](https://github.com/FindingLand/claude-re-skills/raw/main/recrm/recrm.skill)** then install it in Claude: Settings, Capabilities, Skills, upload.
