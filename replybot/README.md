# replybot — Your Anywhere Chatbot

A universal chatbot builder for real estate: turns your AI assistant into one brain that answers new leads in minutes, in your voice, across every platform they reach you on: Furnished Finder, Airbnb, Zillow, TurboTenant, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook Marketplace, your website contact form, email, and text. The reply brain is platform-independent, so when a new platform shows up you classify it (owned inbox, official API, or draft-and-paste marketplace) and the same rules work there too.

You write one policies + property-facts document, the assistant triages each message (answer / needs-you / ignore) and drafts the reply from your real rules on pets, deposits, pricing, and availability. Start in draft-for-approval mode, move to auto-send per channel once you trust it. Lead conversion runs up to 5x to 21x higher when you respond in under 5 minutes instead of an hour, so speed is the whole point.
