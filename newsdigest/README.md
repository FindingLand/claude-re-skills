# newsdigest — Your Daily Real Estate Market Brief

Turns Claude into a real estate market editor that works for your attention, not against it. You tell it your metro, your strategy, and the topics you follow (rates and Fed moves, local inventory and prices, the rental and mid-term market, policy and zoning, insurance, new supply, proptech). It searches across three windows (today, this week, this month), cuts the clickbait and the ten-articles-about-one-thing noise, and hands you a short list of headlines only.

The difference: it ships pre-loaded with real estate knowledge, so it reads every headline through YOUR strategy. A flipper, a landlord, an agent, and a wholesaler see the same rate move differently. Say a number and it tells you what that headline means for you specifically, not a generic "this affects the housing market."

No feed, no doomscroll. When nothing important happened in your market, it says so instead of padding. On first run it asks your metro, strategy, and holding types, then saves it so it never asks again.

**[Download newsdigest.skill](https://github.com/FindingLand/claude-re-skills/raw/main/newsdigest/newsdigest.skill)** then install it in Claude: Settings, Capabilities, Skills, upload.
