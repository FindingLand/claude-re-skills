# followup — The Follow-Up Machine

Turns your AI assistant into a complete lead follow-up system so no lead ever dies of silence. It builds the sequences (new lead, went-quiet, past client, sphere), tells you exactly who to contact today and with what, drafts every message in your voice, keeps the log, and re-engages your dead pile.

Fixes the number one leak in real estate: most leads get 1 to 2 attempts and then get dropped, while a deal usually takes 8 or more touches over weeks. You run it in about 15 minutes a day and nobody gets forgotten.
