# rentbill — Utility Billing Assistant

For landlords and property managers who pass utilities through to tenants. Your AI assistant organizes every utility bill in one place (water, electric, gas, trash), calculates each tenant's share (flat, per-unit split, or RUBS-style), and writes the exact charges and tenant notices for you to enter in whatever you collect rent with: TurboTenant, Avail, Apartments.com, Zillow Rental Manager, or a simple ledger.

Nothing forgotten, billed on time every month, with a clean paper trail per tenant for disputes and taxes. Includes the allocation math with worked examples, late and move-out proration, and copy-paste prompt templates.
