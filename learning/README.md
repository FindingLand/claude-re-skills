# learning: One Lesson a Day About How Work Actually Works

A short daily lesson on productivity, focus and how work actually gets done: one idea, the mechanism
behind it (why it works, not just what to do), and one action sized for today. Say "more" and it goes
deep: full mechanism, the dial settings, the failure modes, a seven-day protocol. Say a lesson was weak
and it tunes itself so that kind of lesson never comes back.

Covers lowering your stimuli baseline so work becomes interesting again (staring at a wall, no phone,
no games, no series), protecting attention, starting when you do not feel like it, making work fun with
mini goals and one ambitious goal, energy and recovery, deciding what not to do, and thinking better.
Sixty-five lessons in the library, a log so nothing repeats, and no invented research.
